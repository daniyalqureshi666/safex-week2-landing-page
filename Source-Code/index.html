Frequently Asked Questions

What is VAPT? expand_more

VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing) combines automated scanning with manual, expert-led exploitation to find and validate security weaknesses across your applications, networks, and cloud infrastructure.

How often should testing be performed? expand_more

We recommend a VAPT at least quarterly, or whenever a major deployment, infrastructure change, or new integration occurs, to stay ahead of evolving threats.

How long does testing take? expand_more

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. A typical single application or network engagement takes about one to two weeks, from kickoff through the final report.

Will testing affect production? expand_more

We plan every engagement carefully with you, favoring staging environments where possible, and using controlled, rate-limited testing techniques in production to minimize any risk of disruption.

Do you provide remediation support? expand_more

Yes. Every report includes a prioritized remediation roadmap, and our team is available to advise your developers during fixes and through the final retest.

What is the difference between VA and PT? expand_more

Vulnerability Assessment (VA) is a scan that identifies known vulnerabilities. Penetration Testing (PT) goes further, actively attempting to exploit those vulnerabilities to show how deep an attacker could realistically go.
